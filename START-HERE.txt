B2B Studio for the Canon EOS 5D Mark III  (beta 3)
Canon EOS 5D Mark III, CANON FIRMWARE 1.2.3 ONLY.  Experimental software: use at your own risk.

WHAT IT DOES
Shoot normally (JPEG). Every picture is baked in the background with a film look, using a 3D colour table, and saved
as a new JPEG in the PIXELJPG folder on the card. Your Canon originals in DCIM are never touched. A film-style
interface (canister shelf, Home, Images, Develop) runs on the camera's screen. This release comes with the 72
film looks of the M-Film project and one extra preset, Cold Vibes.

WHAT YOU NEED
- A 5D Mark III on Canon firmware 1.2.3 that already runs Magic Lantern for the 5D3.123 (install Magic Lantern first,
  from magiclantern.fm / the reticulatedpines "magiclantern_simplified" builds, and check that it starts).
- A card with room for the baked copies. Use JPEG (Large, Medium or Small).
- BACK UP THE CARD first.

INSTALL
1. Camera off. Copy autoexec.bin and the ML folder from this zip to the root of the card that already boots
   Magic Lantern, replacing files with the same name. Do not run "Firmware Update" again.
2. Switch the camera on. If you have Start screen on (System page), the film shelf opens; otherwise press the
   TRASH button on the shooting screen for Home.
3. Pick a look (Film), then shoot. Open Develop to watch the bake queue and Images to look at the results.
To go back: restore your card backup.

BUTTONS (shooting screen)  TRASH = Home, PLAY = Images.   Settings are under Home > System, Viewfinder and Lens.
If the camera does not start, take the battery and card out, then start it without the card.

WHAT TO EXPECT
Baking one picture takes tens of seconds (the camera's processor is slow); shots queue up and are baked one at a
time, and a half-press pauses the bake. The review after a shot is a quick preview, not the finished picture.

PROBLEMS
System > Debug log writes ML/FILMROLL.LOG. Attach it when you report a problem. System > Self-test writes speed numbers to it.
